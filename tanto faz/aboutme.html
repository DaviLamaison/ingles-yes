About Me

My name is Ivan Kaminagakura, and I am a psychologist with a career spanning nearly two decades. I graduated in Psychology in 2006 and pursued my Master's degree at the University of São Paulo (USP), one of Brazil's most renowned academic institutions. Throughout my professional journey, I have dedicated myself to understanding human behavior and supporting individuals in their personal development and emotional well-being. My experience combines academic knowledge with years of practical work, allowing me to offer thoughtful and attentive psychological care.
