About MindFix

At MindFix, we believe that mental health care should be accessible, compassionate, and grounded in scientific research. Our team of licensed clinical psychologists and therapists provides a safe, confidential environment where you can explore your thoughts and develop practical strategies for personal growth.

Whether you are dealing with acute stress, relationship difficulties, or long-standing behavioral patterns, we work collaboratively with you to create a personalized path forward.

Our Psychological Services

  • Individual Therapy: One-on-one sessions focused on anxiety, depression, trauma recovery, and self-esteem.
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Structured, goal-oriented therapy aimed at identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns.
  • Couples & Relationship Counseling: Guided support to improve communication, resolve conflict, and strengthen emotional intimacy.
  • Stress & Burnout Management: Practical tools and mindfulness techniques designed for professionals facing workplace exhaustion.
  • Online Teletherapy: Secure, convenient video sessions available from the comfort of your own home.

How It Works

  1. Initial Assessment: Fill out a brief intake form so we can understand your goals, preferences, and background.
  2. Therapist Matching: We pair you with a licensed specialist whose expertise aligns with your specific needs.
  3. Begin Your Sessions: Start your weekly or bi-weekly appointments in-person or online and track your progress over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is everything discussed in therapy confidential?

Yes. All sessions and client records are strictly confidential and protected under healthcare privacy laws, with standard legal exceptions regarding immediate safety.

How long does a typical session last?

Standard individual and couples therapy sessions last 50 minutes.

Do you accept health insurance?

We work with several major insurance providers and also offer sliding-scale out-of-pocket rates. Contact us to verify your coverage.
